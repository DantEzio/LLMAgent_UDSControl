# Event_06 Rainfall Warning Information

> **Event**: 2016-06-30 Orange Rainstorm Warning — First of 2016
> **Rainfall Time Window**: 2016-06-29 02:00 ~ 2016-07-04 07:00 (6h pre-rain + full rainfall duration + 48h post-rain)
> **Actual Rainfall Period**: 2016-06-29 08:00 ~ 2016-07-02 07:00
> **Data Source**: CMA (cma.gov.cn), Xinhua News, China.com

> **Historical Significance**: This was the **first Orange Rainstorm Warning** issued by the CMA since the start of the 2016 flood season, marking the onset of the most intense rainfall phase in the middle and lower reaches of the Yangtze River.

## Characteristics of This Round

- **Leapfrog Warning Upgrade**: Within less than 8 hours, the warning escalated directly from Yellow to Orange (first of the year), reflecting a sharp increase in rainfall intensity
- **Outbreak After Brief Intermission**: Only 1 day after the warning was lifted on June 29, it was re-issued and rapidly upgraded on the 30th as the Meiyu front intensified again
- **Severe Convection Accompaniment**: Concurrent Blue Severe Convective Weather Warning issued, with hourly rainfall intensity reaching 60–90 mm
