## Disaster Context

As of July 2:
- Localized cumulative rainfall in eastern Hubei and central Anhui exceeded 400 mm
- Heavy rainstorm area across Hubei, Anhui, and Jiangsu reached 140,000 km²
- Mountain torrents broke out in Jinzhai, Anhui; Hanshan activated Level II response
- Tongcheng, Anhui experienced extraordinary rainstorm (June 28 – July 2), with maximum point rainfall of 397.7 mm
